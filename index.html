Jiaqi Li 李佳琪

I work on agents, long-context language models, and evaluation & benchmarking.

I am a researcher at the Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence (BIGAI). My research focuses on building capable AI agents and understanding their long-context capabilities, as well as developing principled evaluation frameworks for large language models.

I have served as a reviewer for NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, CVPR, AAAI, etc.
